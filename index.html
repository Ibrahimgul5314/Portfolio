About Me

I am a Final Year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Engineering & Technology (UET), Peshawar, specializing in Mechanical Design, CAD Modeling, and BIM MEP Engineering.

As a BIM Modeler Intern at Khan BIM & Engineering Services (Saudi Arabia), I developed practical knowledge of Autodesk Revit MEP, BIM coordination, HVAC modeling, multidisciplinary collaboration, and project documentation for building services engineering.

At Iceberg Industries, I received technical training in HVAC engineering, covering VRF (LG Multi V5), AHU, FCU, Chilled Water Systems, Chillers, ERV, BMS, load calculations, duct design principles, and AutoCAD MEP workflows used in commercial building projects.

My engineering background is further strengthened through practical training in Aircraft Maintenance at The Peshawar Flying Club, where I gained exposure to aircraft systems, inspection procedures, and maintenance operations.

I am a Certified SOLIDWORKS Associate (CSWA) by Dassault Systèmes, with proficiency in SOLIDWORKS, PTC Creo Parametric, AutoCAD, AutoCAD MEP, and Autodesk Revit MEP. I am currently seeking graduate engineering opportunities across Gulf and Asian countries in Mechanical Design, BIM MEP, HVAC Engineering, and the Oil & Gas sector. My long-term objective is to pursue a Master's degree in Europe and continue building an international engineering career.
